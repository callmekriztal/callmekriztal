**AI & Backend Developer**
-------------------------

From a very young age, I was curious about computers, which eventually led me to pursue a career in tech.

Like most people, I started with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Front-end wasn't for me, so I explored Web3, mobile development, and eventually cybersecurity. Booting Kali Linux gave me enough confidence to hack the Pentagon. Five minutes later, I was googling how to exit Vim.

Cybersecurity wasn't my destination, but it taught me Linux, which became one of my favorite tools. After countless distros, broken systems, and hours of ricing, I finally settled on Manjaro.

Then I found AI. It clicked. Today I enjoy building AI-powered backends, learning new things, and understanding the complexities of both systems and people.
